A cellular telecommunication tower is to be analyzed for a variety of dynamic loads.
A. First to identify its dynamic properties modeled as a SDOF, a lateral force of 10000 lb is
applied at the top of the tower resulting in a horizontal displacement of 1 inch. A cable
attached to the top of the tower is suddenly severed resulting in a free vibration. At the end of
20 complete vibration cycles, the time is 200 sec and the amplitude is 0.02 inch. Find
effective stiffness, effective mass, undamped natural frequency and damping ratio for the
SDOF system.
B. The tower must survive an extreme natural event, such that the imposed loading can be
modeled as a superposition of a harmonic, triangular and square wave loading whose are
characteristics are given as follows:
1) harmonic loading – amplitude= 10kips, period=0.1s and duration=5s,
2) triangular loading – amplitude= 15kips, period=0.12s and duration=4s,
3) square loading – amplitude= 5kips, period=0.15s and duration=5s,
Using Nonlin, find the imposed wave functions and its FFT. Find the response of the system
assuming that the system is linear. Is the tower designed sufficiently well if the design
criterion is a maximum deflection of 0.5 inch.
C. If the tower is not sufficiently well designed, then suggest alternatives to the owner.
D. What will be the response of the original system if the tower has a yield point is 0.5kips and
the secondary stiffness is 1 kip/in. What is the permanent deflection of the tower. Will the
tower survive this event.
E. The tower is also subjected to an impact loading of 1000 kips of duration 0.05s. What will
be the response, first assuming linear behavior and, subsequently, assuming nonlinear
behavior.
